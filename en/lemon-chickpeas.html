🫘 SUMMER CHICKPEAS WITH LEMON AND ROSEMARY 🌅

Ingredients for 4 people:
  • 400g pre-cooked chickpeas (drained and rinsed)
  • 2 untreated lemons
  • 3 fresh rosemary sprigs
  • 4 tablespoons EVO oil
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1/2 teaspoon sweet chili pepper
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Fresh mint for garnish
Preparation:
Heat 3 tablespoons of extra virgin olive oil in a non-stick pan over medium-low heat with crushed garlic cloves and finely chopped fresh rosemary, letting it infuse for 2-3 minutes until the aroma is released. Add the 400g of drained chickpeas and let them absorb the flavors over medium heat for 5-6 minutes, stirring gently to prevent them from breaking. Remove from heat and add the grated lemon zest and freshly squeezed lemon juice, a drizzle of raw extra virgin olive oil, and sweet chili pepper. Stir gently to distribute the aromas evenly, then transfer to the refrigerator for at least an hour: this resting period is essential as it allows the flavors to meld together perfectly. Serve in terracotta bowls garnished with fresh mint leaves and, if desired, some toasted almond slivers for a crunchy touch.

💬 Notes and Tips

These summer chickpeas with lemon and rosemary come from the Mediterranean tradition that knows how to enhance the simplicity of quality ingredients. This dish smells of summer and sunsets over the sea, when the sun begins to paint the horizon in shades of orange and pink.

Variation: For an even more aromatic touch, add a few bay leaves while heating the oil, removing them before serving. Bay leaf is one of the aromatics that most increases engagement on @leguminando! 🌿

Serving suggestion: This dish is perfect for a fresh lunch on a terrace overlooking the sea. Serve with a glass of light white wine and whole grain bread crostini. 🌅
